Permutation Game For Student
Focus Break Drag and Swap
Logic Puzzle

Match the hidden permutation in as few swaps as possible.

Drag one star onto another to swap positions. After each move, the game shows how many stars are already in the correct place, so you can refine your next guess.

How To Play

Each star represents one unique letter from A to H. Your visible order and the hidden target order are both shuffled. Keep swapping until all 8 positions are correct.

Quick Hints

Start broad

Test a few different positions early instead of forcing one star immediately.

Watch the counter

If the correct-position count rises, one or both moved stars likely improved.

Keep notes short

Small clues like "A maybe slot 3" are enough to guide your next move.
